# Keyless fixture STT coordinator

Slice on PR27: a trusted in-process coordinator consumes the existing private claim and invokes only the keyless synthetic STT registry. No route, vendor registration, credential loading, schema change or existing-profile migration. Generated tones remain lookup fixtures, not ASR.

`run(owner,id,parentSignal?)` uses current server roles at claim, then the pinned fixture provider. It returns saved admission metadata/outcome only, never audio/token/raw error. Duplicate/terminal/foreign/blocked claims cannot invoke another adapter. A pre-aborted request cancels without invocation. Clamp the execution deadline to the durable claim deadline; propagate parent/cancel/deadline aborts cooperatively, with no retry/fallback.

Important: a bounded wait ending does not prove an adapter stopped. Retain the durable pending slot until the actual adapter promise acknowledges, then settle a fixed timeout/aborted/provider_failed outcome or validated completed evidence. Late text after cancellation/revocation/expiry remains suppressed by the existing authoritative settlement boundary. A trusted `drain()` may await acknowledgements for orderly shutdown/testing; it has no timeout guarantee and is not a public request. An abort-ignoring adapter can retain a slot until acknowledgement or exclusive restart. Surface acknowledgement storage failures through drain without raw provider detail; no background retry/replay.

Cancellation through the coordinator first commits owned cancellation then signals active work. Direct store media revocation suppresses output atomically but has no cancellation event bus yet; do not claim it recalls bytes or immediately stops a running adapter. Public shutdown/integration wiring is separate.
